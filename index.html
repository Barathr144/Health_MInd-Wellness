Holistic Healing | Scientific Care | Personalized Wellness

Welcome to Mindful Wellness Clinic, where traditional healing wisdom meets modern scientific healthcare. We believe that true wellness begins by identifying and addressing the root cause of disease rather than simply managing symptoms.

Our integrated healthcare approach combines Functional Medicine, Lifestyle Medicine, Ayurveda, Naturopathy, Yoga Therapy, Ozone Therapy, Physiotherapy, Mind-Body Medicine, and Advanced Regenerative Therapies to support complete physical, mental, and emotional well-being.

Under the guidance of Dr. Subbiah R, BNYS, PGAC, Lifestyle & Functional Medicine Consultant, we provide personalized, evidence-informed care designed to restore health naturally and sustainably.
